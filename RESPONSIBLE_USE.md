# Responsible use

Lecture Scribe is a tool for **personal study**. You are responsible for how you use it.

1. **Get permission before you record.** Many lecturers, schools and course rules require consent or forbid recording. Ask first, and follow your institution's policy. Recording is not always a right, even if you are enrolled.
2. **The law differs by country and region.** Some places let you record a conversation if you take part in it, others require everyone's consent, and rules for recording in a classroom, or for other people's personal data, vary. This project cannot tell you what applies to you. If you are unsure, ask your institution.
3. **Study notes, not publishing.** Keep recordings and transcripts for your own revision. Do not share, post, sell or redistribute lecture content. Lectures are usually the lecturer's or institution's intellectual property, and sharing may breach copyright or your institution's rules.
4. **Think about other people.** Recordings can contain classmates and personal information. Delete recordings you no longer need, and do not keep or share what you do not need.
5. **Accommodations are different.** If you need recordings because of a disability, talk to your institution's accessibility or disability service. They can arrange permission and may offer their own tools.
6. **AI output can be wrong.** Speech recognition mishears names, numbers and jargon, and AI tools can invent facts. Check anything that matters against the lecture, your textbook and your course materials. Do not use AI output to cheat or to break your institution's academic-integrity rules.
7. **Not affiliated.** Lecture Scribe is an independent open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by any university, school, or the makers of Chrome, Whisper, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or any other third party named in this repository.
